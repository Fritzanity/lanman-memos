Privacy policy

Fritz Lanman AI Projects · effective November 11, 2026

These are private, invite-only apps run by Fritz Lanman (Backstory, Cockpit and his personal tools). This page says what they collect and what happens to it.

What we receive from Google

  • Invited family and testers: your name and email address, when you choose "Sign in with Google". We do not read your mail, contacts, calendar, photos or files.
  • Fritz's own account only: his personal tools read his own Gmail, Calendar and Photos, with his permission, to help him. They never request this access from anyone else.

How it is used

  • To check that you were invited, and to keep you signed in.
  • To show your name next to things you add, such as a story you record in Backstory.

What you add yourself

In Backstory, photos, videos and voice recordings that you or your relatives add are kept so the invited family can see and hear them. Original recordings are preserved.

Where it is kept and who sees it

  • Data is stored on computers Fritz owns and on backups he controls.
  • Sign-in passes through Cloudflare and Google, which handle it under their own privacy policies.
  • It is visible only to Fritz and the people he has invited to the same app.
  • It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone else. Google user data is not used to train general-purpose AI models.

Keeping and deleting

Data is kept while you have access. You can ask Fritz at any time to remove your access, your sign-in details, or anything you added, and it will be deleted, including from backups as they roll over. You can also remove the app's access from your Google account settings.

Contact

Ask Fritz directly; everyone with access was invited by him personally.
