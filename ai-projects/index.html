Fritz Lanman AI Projects

Private, invite-only apps built and run by Fritz Lanman for himself, his family and a few invited testers.

These apps are not open to the public. You can only sign in if Fritz has invited you. Sign-in uses "Sign in with Google" so you do not need another password.

Backstory
A family archive. Relatives look through shared photos and videos and record the stories behind them in their own voices.
Cockpit
Fritz's own workspace for keeping track of his projects.
Personal tools
Assistants that work only with Fritz's own Google account, at his direction.

What signing in shares

When you sign in with Google, the app receives your name and email address, and uses them only to check that you were invited and to show who added a story. Nothing else in your Google account is read.
